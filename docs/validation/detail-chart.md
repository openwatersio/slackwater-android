# Detail chart validation

The scene uses one centred time coordinate at 18 dp per elapsed hour. The Kotlin chart-model tests exercise the same water series and Almanac code as the Android Canvas. The device conformance harness opens real station pages for Friday Harbor, a NOAA current, Malibu Rapids, a rotary current at slack, and a long station name. Instrumentation writes a PNG for each station and screen size.

Run the JVM chart and timeline regressions with `./gradlew :core:test --tests '*timeline*' :app:testDebugUnitTest --tests '*ChartGeometryTest'`. Run the station captures with `./gradlew :app:connectedDebugAndroidTest -Pandroid.testInstrumentationRunnerArguments.class=io.openwaters.slackwater.android.detail.DetailConformanceTest`.

## Frame and worker ownership

The lead and fixed riding dot use the selected instant. A decoded engine model is sampled at one-second precision on a background worker. While the evaluator runs, the lead uses the loaded series at the newly selected instant. A result from another instant, model, unit or source cannot replace it. Each evaluation stream retains one running request and conflates newer selections. Astronomy searches for eclipses and rise/set arcs use the published interval once. Star twinkle redraws the current positions at most eight times per second and pauses in daylight and under Reduce Motion.

The fixed-time phone montage revealed a dark lunar disc under a tide lead and low contrast for hanging values. The renderer now masks celestial detail within the measured lead, uses foam ink and a dark shadow for hanging values, and fades day/night shading vertically behind the time rows.

The resting Friday Harbor phone capture parks at the 2:10 PM high. The lead and hanging reading both show 7.7 ft, the Now control sits to the left, and the centred commentary gives sunset in 2 h 34 min, matching the plotted 4:44 PM sunset. Sun and moon clear the lead, and the past and future strokes separate at the white absolute-now dot. TalkBack spoke the station-zone abbreviation PST.
